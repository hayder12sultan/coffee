Our story

Coffee with a ritual and a sense of place.

Buna Bet began with a simple idea: coffee should be slow, honest, and deeply human. We source from some of Ethiopia’s most respected farms and roast in small batches so every cup tastes like the soil, altitude, and care behind it.

From the first crack of the roast to the final pour over a warm table, we celebrate coffee as hospitality. We invite guests to linger, share stories, and discover flavors that feel both familiar and new.
